2. Page title heading and tab panel
-----------------------------------
agent-desktop snapshot (Settings > General, then Settings > Usage):
    tabpanel 'General'   ['focused']
      unknown 'General'
    ...
    tabpanel 'Usage'   ['focused']
      unknown 'Usage'
Raw AX attributes read with a small AXUIElement probe (axroles.swift):
    AXGroup [group] title=Usage
      Heading [Heading] title=Usage
The heading node is present and named after the page, and the panel is a tab
panel named after its tab. On macOS the role reaches clients as the
non-standard AXRole "Heading": accesskit_macos 0.26.3 maps Role::Heading to the
string "Heading" instead of AXHeading and drops the level, which is why
agent-desktop reports "unknown". Upstream fix pending: AccessKit PR #830
"feat(macos): Expose heading levels and table structure" (open). AT-SPI and UIA
map Role::Heading natively.
